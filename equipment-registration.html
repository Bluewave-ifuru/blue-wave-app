The use of a dive computer is compulsory for each diver, student or participant. Divers must commence their safety-stop with a tank pressure not less than 50 bars and surface with minimum 30 bars left in the tank. No solo diving is permitted. Remain throughout the dive with your dive buddy. The maximum bottom time is 60 minutes including the safety stop. All dives are under guidance of a Dive Master or Instructor.
